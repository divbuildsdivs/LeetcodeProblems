LeetCode 42 - Trapping Rain Water
---------------------------------

Logic:
1. Use two pointers:
   - left starts at index 0
   - right starts at index n - 1

2. Track the highest wall seen so far from both sides:
   - leftMax
   - rightMax

3. While left < right:
   - If leftMax <= rightMax:
     - Move left one step forward
     - Update leftMax = max(leftMax, height[left])
     - Water trapped at left = leftMax - height[left]

   - Else:
     - Move right one step backward
     - Update rightMax = max(rightMax, height[right])
     - Water trapped at right = rightMax - height[right]

Why this works:
- Water trapped at any index depends on the smaller of:
  - maximum height on the left
  - maximum height on the right
- If leftMax <= rightMax, then the left side is the limiting boundary,
  so the trapped water at the current left position can be computed safely.
- Similarly, if rightMax < leftMax, then the right side is the limiting boundary.

Example intuition:
- A bar can hold water only if there is a taller boundary on both sides.
- The amount stored is:
  min(maxLeft, maxRight) - height[i]
- The two-pointer approach avoids precomputing max arrays by updating
  boundaries as the pointers move inward.

Time Complexity:
O(n)

Space Complexity:
O(1)
